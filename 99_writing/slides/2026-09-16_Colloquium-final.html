The Case of Generative AI

Generative AI…

  • permeated consumers’, marketers’, and researchers’ lifes rapidly and profoundly (Cillo & Rubera, 2025; Grewal et al., 2025)
  • changes how consumers (will) interact with technology (Mogaji & Jain, 2024; Muzumdar et al., 2025)
  • changes the research process itself (Gartenberg et al., 2026)
  • rapid increase in publications on Generative AI (Riandhi et al., 2025)
  • … and with Generative AI(Gartenberg et al., 2026)
  • Special issues and focus in top journals (Bart et al., 2026; Wang, 2026)

\(\Rightarrow\) Is Generative AI as a new consumer technology different from what our field has studied before?
